THE BIG PICTURE

About the program

Yale Young Global Scholars is a residential pre-college program run by Yale University's Office of International Affairs. Each summer, high school students from across the world spend two weeks on Yale's campus in an academic track centered on science and technology, politics and economics, or global challenges.

The days combine Yale faculty lectures with smaller seminars led by undergraduate and graduate instructors. Students test ideas with peers, collaborate on projects and experience the social side of university life in residential colleges. The program is designed to make global conversation a central part of academic learning.

Admission is competitive, and eligibility is specific to the 2027 cycle. Students must be current high school sophomores or juniors and meet age, graduation and English fluency requirements. Need-based financial aid is available to applicants from all countries; applicants should review the official deadlines and aid instructions before submitting.

THE JOURNEY

How it came to life

A brief look at the moments that shaped YYGS.

2001

Ivy Scholars begins

Yale faculty and administrators started a small pre-collegiate summer program to bring future leaders together across borders.

2012

A global identity

The program was renamed Yale Young Global Scholars to reflect its international conversation and ambition.

2016

Experiments beyond New Haven

YYGS offered sessions at Yale-NUS College in Singapore and Yale Center Beijing in China.

2023

Back on campus

After three online summers during the pandemic, YYGS returned to in-person programming at Yale.
