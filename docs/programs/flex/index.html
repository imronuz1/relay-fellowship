THE BIG PICTURE

About the program

FLEX is a competitive exchange scholarship for high school students from participating countries in Europe and Eurasia. The U.S. Department of State funds the program. Selected students spend a school year in the United States, attend an American high school and live with a volunteer host family.

The exchange is built around mutual understanding. Students share their culture with American classmates and neighbors while learning how another community studies, celebrates, serves and solves problems. The host family, school and local organization form a support network throughout the year.

FLEX selection is demanding and country-specific. The U.S. Department of State describes a multi-stage public application process. Eligibility and deadlines change by country, so an applicant should use the official FLEX site for the current local round rather than relying on a universal deadline.

THE JOURNEY

How it came to life

A brief look at the moments that shaped FLEX.

1992

A new exchange vision

The U.S. FREEDOM Support Act created a framework for educational exchange with countries emerging after the Soviet Union's dissolution.

1993

FLEX begins

The Future Leaders Exchange program launched to strengthen long-term ties and mutual understanding between the United States and Europe and Eurasia.

Today

An enduring alumni network

The Department of State reports more than 32,000 FLEX alumni across 24 countries over the program's history.
